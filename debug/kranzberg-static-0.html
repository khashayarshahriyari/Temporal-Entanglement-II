Upcoming Events

Tickets/Box Office

All advance tickets for productions taking place at a Kranzberg Arts Foundation venue can be purchased via any MetroTix outlet, or online at www.metrotix.com. Walk-up sales are available at The Fabulous Fox Theatre Box Office, The Fox Box Office is only open on show days.

Fabulous Fox Box Office hours on show days:
  •   For evening performances – 4pm until ½ hour after the show begins
•   For 2pm matinee performances – Noon until ½ hour after the final show of the day begins
•   For 1pm matinee performances – 11am until ½ hour after the final show of the day begins

MetroTix charge by phone 314.534.1111    Phone lines open 10am-8pm – 7 days a week 

MetroTix operates a robust mobile and tablet web platform, and patrons will have the option of mail, print your own, or will-call delivery of tickets. Pending availability, tickets can also be purchased on-site when doors open, which is one hour before every production.

Venue Rules

For specific questions regarding venue and event rules and information, contact the show booker or producer, specific event information can also be found through event ticketing platform – MetroTix. For all KAF venues, purses and small bags are allowed in to all venues. No outside food or drink is allowed. No backpacks are allowed at The Big Top.
